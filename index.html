03 / The comparison method

A better list starts with better questions.

Use this Wheebuy shopping guide to turn scattered bookmarks into a comparison you can actually use.

Keep dated notes. Product pages, options and charges can change between discovery and checkout.

  1. Choose the purpose

    Define the category, intended use, preferred fit and maximum total budget before collecting links.

  2. Capture the exact listing

    Save the seller, source URL, selected variant, listed currency and the date you checked the page.

  3. Compare measurable details

    Use garment dimensions, shoe measurements where available, material descriptions and relevant photographs.

  4. Review the complete cost

    Include applicable delivery, service, payment, currency conversion and destination charges before deciding.

Fit notes

Measure something you already wear.

  • T-shirts and hoodies: compare chest width, body length, shoulder width and sleeve length.
  • Pants and shorts: check waist, rise, thigh width and inseam using the same measurement method.
  • Shoes: review the model-specific chart and any available foot-length guidance.
  • Headwear and jewelry: confirm circumference, ring size, chain length or other relevant dimensions.

Photo notes

Look beyond the first picture.

  • Check that photographs show the selected model, color and variant.
  • Review seams, closures, soles, labels and finish where visible.
  • Treat lighting and display color as possible sources of variation.
  • Use photographs to inspect visible details; they cannot establish authenticity or guarantee performance.

05 / Clear answers

Before you follow a find.

Direct answers to common questions about spreadsheet browsing and product comparisons.

What is a Wheebuy spreadsheet?

A Wheebuy spreadsheet is a product-link list intended to help shoppers organize and compare items for a Wheebuy-related shopping workflow. Useful columns include product URLs, listed prices, measurements and review notes. A spreadsheet entry alone does not verify authenticity, stock or seller reliability.

Does this page provide a downloadable spreadsheet?

No. This page provides a browsing guide, a comparison checklist and links to CAFANS categories. It does not host an XLSX file, a Google Sheet or a verified live Wheebuy product feed.

Where do the category links lead?

All category links lead to the corresponding CAFANS category. General browsing links lead to the CAFANS homepage.

How should I compare clothing sizes?

Compare the seller's garment measurements with a similar item you already own. Check the measurement method, units and stated tolerance instead of relying only on labels such as medium or large.

How do I estimate the total order cost?

Start with the item price, then add applicable domestic delivery, service charges, international shipping, payment or currency conversion costs, and destination taxes or duties. Confirm current charges before paying.

Do brand names or photographs prove that an item is authentic?

No. Brand names, listing titles and photographs are not proof of authenticity or authorization. Check the actual seller, product documentation, purchase terms and any available independent authentication.
